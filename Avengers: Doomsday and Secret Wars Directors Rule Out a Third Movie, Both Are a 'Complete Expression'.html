The architects behind the upcoming **Avengers: Doomsday** and **Avengers: Secret Wars**, the legendary Russo Brothers, have dropped a bombshell: there will be NO third movie after Secret Wars!

⚡ No More Multiverse Mayhem? Russo Brothers Shut Down Avengers Trilogy Talk! ⚡

That's right, folks! Forget those fan theories about a secret third flick to wrap it all up. The Russos, speaking from the electric atmosphere of SXSW London, have made it crystal clear: **Avengers: Secret Wars** is the grand finale for their current MCU saga. They're calling it a "complete expression" of the story they've been building, and frankly, we're here for that focused intensity!

🎯 A Complete Vision, Not an Endless Grind! 🎯

Anthony Russo laid it out, stating, "The stories that we're focused on now are complete." He elaborated that "Doomsday and Secret Wars speak with one another as two films and they form a complete expression." While they're not closing the door on future collaborations with Marvel, their current mission is locked and loaded on these two colossal events. No stretching the narrative thin, just pure, unadulterated epicness!

💥 The Big Picture: Doomsday and Secret Wars as One! 💥

So, what does this mean for you, the dedicated fans who've been dissecting every trailer and rumor? It means Marvel is committed to delivering a powerful, cohesive conclusion. The sheer number of characters rumored to be involved in these films could have easily spiraled into a never-ending saga, but the Russos are opting for a sharp, impactful ending. This isn't about milking the cash cow; it's about crafting a definitive chapter. The speculation about splitting Secret Wars into two parts? Utterly debunked!
